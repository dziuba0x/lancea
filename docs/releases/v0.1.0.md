The first release of Lancea: a co-signer AI agents cannot talk past, live 24/7 on XRPL testnet and Flare Coston2.

**The guard.** An XRP Ledger account whose SignerList is the principal (weight 2), the agent (1) and the guard (1), with quorum 2. Before the guard co-signs, it:
- asks DELICTI's `SummaMeter` whether the payment fits the umbrella's dollar budget across every rail, priced by the FTSO;
- writes the reservation on Flare before the signature exists;
- reads Flare Smart Accounts instructions against the principal's policy.

An attempt is struck on-chain, and the tripwire then shuts every rail until the principal re-arms it.

**The autopilot and its wheel.** One step every 5 minutes, each one a guarded XRPL payment: mint, deposit into Firelight, withdraw, claim, redeem, and round again. It paces itself by the guard's gas, and the XRPL testnet faucet keeps the account topped up.

**The services.** `lancea-guard`, `lancea-autopilot` and `lancea-feed` run as user services on an Oracle Cloud Always Free machine. The feed goes out every minute to [the dashboard](https://dziuba0x.github.io/lancea/): Lancea Watch, in clear liquid glass over a living sky. A GitHub Actions watchman reads the feed every half hour.

**Proven live:** the leash on DELICTI's deployed meter (6 of 6 decisions as designed), the drill (a staged hijack struck, and the re-arm), and the wheel. The runs are in [docs/runs.md](https://github.com/dziuba0x/lancea/blob/main/docs/runs.md).
